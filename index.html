04 · How we work

Understand. Design. Build. Evolve.

The same four stages sit behind every system on this site. Discovery is where it starts — a conversation about how the work is done today, before anything is proposed.

Book a discovery session

  1. 01

    Understand

    We learn your business before proposing anything: how the work is actually done, where it breaks, and what a fix is worth.

  2. 02

    Design

    We design the system around your people and your process, and test it with them before anything is built.

  3. 03

    Build

    Short, demonstrated sprints on the Microsoft Cloud, so you see the system grow and steer it as it does.

  4. 04

    Evolve

    Go-live is the start. We monitor, maintain and keep improving the system as the business changes.

07 · Principles

Seven systems. The same four decisions.

The four operating principles behind every system on this site, each with the case studies it is seen in.

  1. 01

    Start where the work leaks, not where the software is.

    Enquiries lost between apps at SBS. Quotes, terms and payments chased by hand at WasteMart. We fix the leak before we design a screen.

    Seen in SBS Waterproofing WasteMart

  2. 02

    Capture it once, where it happens, even with no signal.

    Sonke’s facilitators log sessions offline. HomeAssist plumbers photograph each step on site. WasteMart drivers confirm with a scan.

    Seen in Sonke Gender Justice HomeAssist WasteMart

  3. 03

    Automate the hand-off nobody owns.

    SBS deposit invoices go straight into accounting. WasteMart operations are alerted the moment payment lands. Tore’s claims are checked against the sessions actually run.

    Seen in SBS Waterproofing WasteMart Tore's Foundation

  4. 04

    Build for year ten, not launch day.

    SBS still runs on the system from 2015. Trans Hex’s version two added forecasts and live exchange rates. The SBS system grew into ManaGEM.

    Seen in SBS Waterproofing Trans Hex Group ManaGEM

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Still have a question? Ask us on WhatsApp or book a 20-minute demo. Real people, South African hours. Chat on WhatsApp
01Does ManaGem work with Xero and Sage?

Yes. ManaGem syncs invoices, customers and payments with Xero and Sage cloud accounting. You keep your accounting system and your accountant keeps their workflow; ManaGem runs everything before the invoice.

02How long does setup take?

Most businesses are quoting from ManaGem within a week. Upload your existing quotes, price list and customer list, and ManaGem builds your products, price rules and customers for you to check. If you would rather have a hand, our team does the setup with you.

03Can I move my data from spreadsheets?

Yes. Customer lists, price lists and product sheets in Excel or CSV import directly. Old quotes in PDF or Word can be uploaded so ManaGem can learn your products and pricing from them.

04Can ManaGem be customised for my business?

Yes. ManaGem is built and supported in South Africa, so changes to workflows, documents and reports are quoted and delivered by the same team, usually in weeks rather than the months a traditional ERP takes.

05What happens after the free trial?

After 30 days you choose a plan and carry on; everything you set up during the trial stays. If you do not continue, your account closes and you can export your data. No card is needed to start.

06Where is my data stored, and is it secure?

Your data is stored in secure, backed-up cloud infrastructure, encrypted in transit and at rest, with access controlled per user. ManaGem is run in line with POPIA.

07Can my factory staff use it on a phone or tablet?

Yes. Job cards, phase updates and the floor dashboard work on any phone or tablet browser. Shop-floor users are priced separately so you can give everyone on the floor a login.

08How do SMS credits work?

Customer updates by SMS use credits that you top up from your dashboard. Email updates are included in every plan.

09Is there a contract?

No lock-in. Plans are month to month and you can cancel any time. Annual plans are paid up front in exchange for two months free. Founding customers who choose to lock their price keep it for three years.

10Do you offer training and support?

Yes. Every plan includes onboarding, help articles and support by email, phone and WhatsApp during South African business hours from a team you can actually call.

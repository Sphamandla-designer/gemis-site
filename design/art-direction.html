GEMIS · REDESIGN · ART DIRECTION SUMMARY

One source of truth

The whole site is organised around a single object: a cut stone that is whole when the business runs on one system and fractures into four shards when it does not. Everything else — the palette, the type, the motion — exists to make that object credible and to stay out of its way.

Palette

The existing palette is kept in full. Four colours are added for the gem; one of them, the favicon's blue, is the only colour allowed to mean "live".

Type scale

Archivo, with its width axis: headlines enter at 88 and settle at 100. JetBrains Mono for figures, labels and the spine. One scale, set in tokens.css, used by every page.

The gem — three key frames

Procedural, in three.js: a pentagon table, a decagonal girdle, a pavilion to a point — the favicon's silhouette in glass. Built as four wedges (Sales, Stock, Payroll, Projects) so the same object can fracture and lock back together. These stills are also the reduced-motion and no-WebGL fallbacks.

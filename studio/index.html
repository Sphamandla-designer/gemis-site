Your alternatives

You have five options. Four of them aren't us.

We would rather you chose the right one than the nearest one. Here is the honest version.

A freelance designer

Often excellent, and usually cheaper. You carry the risk of availability, and the work stops at the design file unless you have developers waiting.

A design agency

Deeper bench and a broader brand offer. Expect a proposal process, a larger minimum, and a separate conversation with whoever builds it.

A development shop

They will build exactly what you specify. The question they can't answer is whether what you specified is what your users needed.

Hiring in-house

The right answer once there is enough design work to fill a full week, every week. Until then it is roughly R600,000 a year and a recruitment cycle ahead of the work.

Doing nothing

Entirely reasonable — provided you know what it costs. The support load, the workarounds and the churn continue, and they compound quietly. A Teardown at R6,500 is the cheapest way to find out whether they are worth fixing.

Questions

Before you ask us.

Are prices excluding VAT?

Yes. GEM Information Systems is a registered VAT vendor, and every price published on this site excludes VAT. The VAT amount is shown on your invoice.

What if we already have a developer?

That is the normal case, and nothing changes. Developer handoff is included in every engagement — specifications, states and assets your team can build from. We are equally happy handing over as building.

Who owns the files and the IP?

Ownership of the design files and the work delivered transfers to you. The specific terms — including revision rounds and scope boundaries — are set out in the engagement terms, which you receive before you commit to anything.

What if the Teardown says we don't need a redesign?

Then that is the finding, and it is a useful one. You keep the ranked issue list, the five redesigned screens and the roadmap, and you have spent R6,500 instead of committing to a project you did not need.

Can you build as well as design, and does that cost extra?

Yes. GEMIS Studio is the design practice of GEM Information Systems, a software company — so design and build sit under one roof. Build is scoped and priced separately from design, but the handoff between them costs you nothing, because there isn't one.

How quickly can you start?

The Teardown session is held within three working days of booking, and findings follow five days from that point. Larger engagements are scheduled against current capacity, which we tell you before you commit.

Do you work outside South Africa?

Yes. The work is remote by default and the sessions are held online. Pricing is published in South African Rand and invoiced in Rand.

What happens after the 30 days of aftercare?

Nothing expires that you depend on — the work is yours. If you want continuing design capacity after that month, the Embedded Designer retainer is built for it; otherwise we are available for a further engagement when you need one.

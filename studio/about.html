Our mission

Technology should move your business forward — so we design the screen and build what we design.

×
We never sell a stage.
Developer handoff in the price.
Thirty days of aftercare.

Technology should never become another problem to manage. It should simplify operations, empower your teams, eliminate inefficiencies and give leaders the insight to decide with confidence.

Most studios hand over a file and leave. We sit inside a systems company, so what we design has to survive engineering, procurement and the first month of real users.

Every engagement is led by a senior product designer — the same person who scopes the work reviews every screen that leaves the studio. No handoffs to juniors, no account layer between you and the person designing your product.

Our values

The foundation of

Great software begins with understanding people. These four principles shape how we scope, build and support every system we put into production — and why our clients stay with us long after go-live.

01People
Built around people

Before a single line of code, we learn your workflows, processes and goals — so the result is software people actually enjoy using.

02Scale
Built for scale

Businesses evolve, and your software should evolve with them — growth without rebuilding the systems you rely on every day.

03Reliability

04Partnership

The company behind the studio

A decade of systems
that matter.

For over a decade, GEM Information Systems has partnered with businesses, NGOs and enterprise organisations to design, build and support software that solves complex operational challenges — secure, scalable digital ecosystems built on Microsoft Cloud.

  1. 2014
    Founded in Cape Town

    GEM Information Systems opens its doors, building affordable tools for SMEs and non-profits.

  2. Then
    Into the Microsoft Cloud

    Azure, Azure SQL and C# become the backbone — enterprise infrastructure for mission-critical work.

  3. Since
    Platforms at national scale

    ERP, learning, waste, property and community systems serving organisations nationally and abroad.

  4. Today
    A long-term partner

    Discovery through continuous improvement — we stay with the systems we ship.

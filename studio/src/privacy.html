01Who we are

GEMIS® Studio is the product and experience design practice of GEM Information Systems (Pty) Ltd (“GEMIS”, “we”, “us”), the responsible party for the personal information described here.

Questions about this notice, and every request below, go to info@gemis.co.za.

Name the Information Officer registered with the Information Regulator, and confirm the company registration number, before this notice is final.

02What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form:

  • your name and work email address;
  • your company and your product’s web address, if you give them;
  • the service you are asking about, and your message;
  • that you agreed to this notice, and the date.

We do not ask for special personal information (such as health, beliefs or biometric information), and we ask you not to include any in your message.

03How the form works

This site has no server of its own. When you press Send enquiry, your email app opens with a message to info@gemis.co.za, your answers already filled in. Nothing reaches us until you send that email yourself, and the site does not keep a copy of what you typed.

If you write to us directly instead, the same rules apply to your email.

04Why we use it

For one purpose: to reply to your enquiry and, if you go on to work with us, to run that engagement. We do not sell, rent or share your information for anyone’s marketing, and we will not add you to a mailing list without your separate consent.

05Where it goes

Enquiries arrive in a monitored GEMIS mailbox, not a personal address. Only the people who need it to answer you can see it. Our email provider stores that mailbox on our behalf.

Name the email provider and where it stores mail; if outside South Africa, this section must describe the cross-border transfer (POPIA section 72).

06How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within twelve months of our last contact. Records that belong to an engagement are kept for as long as tax and company law require.

07Cookies and analytics

The studio sets no cookies unless you accept analytics. It never uses advertising cookies. Your choice itself is remembered in your browser’s local storage, so we do not ask on every page.

If you accept, we use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are useful: the pages viewed, how you arrived, your device and browser type, and an approximate location (city or country). It sets the cookies _ga and _ga_<id>, which last up to two years. Google signals and advertising features are switched off. Google processes this data for us and may do so outside South Africa.

08Hosting and other sites

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub records technical details of each request, such as your IP address, to run and secure the service, under GitHub’s privacy statement. Our fonts and images are served from this site, not from third parties.

Links to LinkedIn and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

09Your rights

Under POPIA you may:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you, and have it corrected or deleted;
  • object to how we process it, or withdraw your consent at any time;
  • complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa).

Write to info@gemis.co.za first, and we will respond within a reasonable time. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.

10Changes to this notice

If this notice changes, the date at the top changes with it, and if the change affects analytics we will ask for your cookie choice again. The terms of use cover the rest of the site.

How we handle your information.

This notice explains how GEM Information Systems (Pty) Ltd, trading as GEMIS® Studio ("we", "us"), collects and processes personal information submitted through the enquiry form on this site, in terms of the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA).

What we collect

When you submit an enquiry we collect the information you provide: your name, work email address, company name, the service you are interested in, your indicative budget band, and your message. We do not collect any special personal information.

Why we collect it

We process this information for one purpose: to respond to your enquiry and, where you engage us, to administer that engagement. We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for marketing. We will not add you to a mailing list without your separate, explicit consent.

Consent

The enquiry form asks for your explicit consent before submission. Your consent is recorded with your enquiry. You may withdraw it at any time by writing to info@gemis.co.za, and we will delete your information unless we are required to retain it for a live engagement or by law.

Where it goes

Enquiries are delivered to a monitored GEMIS mailbox. They are not delivered to personal addresses. Access is limited to the people who need it to respond to you.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to an engagement are deleted within twelve months of the last contact. Records connected to an engagement are retained for the period required by tax and company law.

Cookies and analytics

This page stores a single session value in your browser to avoid replaying its loading animation. It sets no advertising or tracking cookies. If analytics requiring cookies are introduced, they will be set only after you consent.

Your rights

Under POPIA you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, and you may object to processing or lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator (South Africa). Contact us first at info@gemis.co.za or +27 11 219 5008 and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Terms of engagement

Terms governing engagements — scope, payment, intellectual property and support — are issued with each proposal. This page will link to the standard terms once approved for publication.

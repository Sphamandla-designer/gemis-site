Custom software development

Systems designed around how
your business actually runs.

When the work no longer fits off-the-shelf software, or lives across several tools and spreadsheets, GEMIS designs and builds a system around your processes, your people and the way the business is run.

The situation

When the business has outgrown its tools

Most operations do not lack software. They have several tools that each hold part of the picture, a spreadsheet that holds the rest, and people who spend their day moving information between them.

Signs you may recognise

  • The same customer or job is entered in more than one place.
  • The real figures live in a spreadsheet someone maintains by hand.
  • Staff work around the software rather than with it.
  • Nobody can follow a job from first enquiry to final invoice in one place.

What GEMIS delivers

One system for the work, captured once

The kinds of system GEMIS builds. Most projects combine several.

  1. 01

    Internal management systems

    The everyday work — enquiries, jobs, quotes, stock — in one system instead of several.

  2. 02

    Workflow automation

    Routine steps such as reference numbers, invoices and alerts handled by the system, not by memory.

  3. 03

    Customer & supplier portals

    Outside parties act on their part of the work — accepting a quote, paying, tracking an order — without phoning in.

  4. 04

    Operations platforms

    Scheduling, dispatch and tracking for work spread across sites, vehicles and teams.

  5. 05

    Business portals

    One web entry point where staff, partners or clients reach the information and tasks that concern them.

  6. 06

    Enterprise applications

    Larger systems with role-based access, so each person sees and does what their role requires.

How the engagement works

How a custom system takes shape

The emphasis shifts from project to project; the order of thinking does not.

  1. 01

    Map the work

    How the process is actually done today — on the floor, not on a whiteboard.

  2. 02

    Find the friction

    Where information is retyped, delayed or lost, and what that costs.

  3. 03

    Design with users

    Screens and workflows shaped and checked with the people who will use them.

  4. 04

    Build in stages

    Working software demonstrated as it grows, so the direction can change early.

  5. 05

    Go live and stay

    A careful rollout, then support and improvement from the team that built it.

Questions

Common questions

The questions that come up first about Custom software development. Anything not answered here is worth raising in a discovery conversation.

01How does a custom software project start?+

With discovery: we learn how the work is done today, where it breaks and what a change is worth, before anything is designed or priced. What we find shapes the scope, and you decide whether to go further.

02What does a custom system cost?+

It depends on the scope, so we do not publish prices. A custom build usually costs more up front than a software licence; whether it pays back depends on how much work the current tools are causing. Discovery puts both sides of that into numbers for your business.

03Can it connect to the systems we already use?+

Usually, where those systems offer a way to connect. The system built for SBS Waterproofing passes deposit invoices to accounting, and HomeAssist’s links to insurers and manufacturers. Each connection is assessed during discovery rather than assumed.

04Who owns the system and the code?+

Ownership, licensing and handover are set out in the agreement for each project, so we agree them with you before the build starts.

05What happens after go-live?+

GEMIS can stay on to maintain and improve the system. SBS Waterproofing, HomeAssist and WasteMart have each worked with us for more than ten years.

Our Story

A Decade Of Systems That Matter

For over a decade, GEM Information Systems has partnered with businesses, NGOs and enterprise organisations to design, build and support software that solves complex operational challenges. We don't simply develop applications — we engineer secure, scalable digital ecosystems built on Microsoft Cloud.

  1. 2014

    Founded In Cape Town

    GEM Information Systems opens its doors, building affordable tools for SMEs and non-profits.

  2. Then

    Into The Microsoft Cloud

    Azure, Azure SQL and C# become the backbone — enterprise infrastructure for mission-critical work.

  3. Since

    Platforms At National Scale

    ERP, learning, waste, property and community systems serving organisations nationally and abroad.

  4. Today

    A Long-Term Partner

    Discovery through continuous improvement — we stay with the systems we ship.

Our Approach

Five Disciplines, Every Project

The same sequence carries every engagement from first conversation to lasting value — so you always know where a project stands and what comes next.

  1. 01

    Understanding before building

    Every successful project begins with listening — business goals, operational challenges, user needs and future ambitions, before any solution is proposed.

  2. 02

    Designing for the future

    Modern architectures on Azure, Azure SQL, C#, React, React Native and Power BI — secure, flexible and ready for what comes next.

  3. 03

    Agile development

    Iterative cycles, regular demonstrations and continuous collaboration — solutions shaped by feedback, not assumptions.

  4. 04

    Quality without compromise

    Structured testing, QA, performance validation and user acceptance before deployment. Reliability is never optional.

  5. 05

    Long-term success

    Deployment planning, training, documentation, maintenance and continuous improvement — value that lasts long after launch.
